# Website safety copy — ready for integration

This copy is for the separate website project. It has not been deployed. Publish it together with the version containing these safeguards; do not advertise them for older installers.

## Beside Download and before any interactive preview

**Flashing light and moving patterns:** Vsualize may cause discomfort, headaches, dizziness or seizures. Stop immediately if you notice symptoms. Read the Health & display care information before starting.

Sessions stop after 30 minutes by default. Unlimited is an explicit, remembered choice. Gentler visuals and Eco are optional controls, not safety guarantees.

Buttons: **Download for Windows** · **Health & display care** · **Security**

## Health & display care page

Use the full text of `docs/guides/safety.md`, adapting internal links to the website's routes. Keep its sections about symptoms, OLED care, GPU workload, Unlimited, network activity and limitations together. Link Health & display care and Security from the site footer and download area.

## Preview behavior

Use a static poster until the visitor chooses Start visuals. Display the short warning immediately beside that button; use the app's full first-run notice before animation. Do not autoplay looping video, animated backgrounds or animated GIFs in the hero or download page. Reduced-motion visitors start stopped and may choose Gentler. Provide a keyboard-accessible Stop control and the same finite session default as the app.

## Claims to retain

- Audio analysis stays on the device; the installed app uses network services for updates.
- Eco selects Auto rendering at 30 fps; actual workload varies by hardware and effect.
- Stop clears the app window and ends capture; it does not turn off the monitor.
- Session limits are precautions, not medically established safe viewing limits.

Avoid claims such as “eye-safe,” “seizure-safe,” “prevents burn-in,” “protects your GPU,” or “risk-free.” Have counsel review distribution terms and any disclaimer language for the intended markets; this copy is product guidance, not a legal waiver.
